# AVAHAN Research Plan

## 1. Purpose

Before designing or developing the AVAHAN management system, the current festival organization process must be understood.

The goal is not to immediately decide what features the software should have. The first goal is to understand:

> How is AVAHAN currently organized and managed without the proposed software system?

## 2. Research Objectives

The research aims to understand:

- How AVAHAN is currently planned and organized
- Who is involved in managing the festival
- How participating colleges and students register
- How events, teams, schedules, and venues are managed
- How results and scores are recorded
- How points and overall winners are calculated
- How certificates are generated and distributed
- How announcements and updates are communicated
- Where festival-related data is currently stored
- What problems exist in the current process

## 3. Research Scope

The research will focus on the operational and information-management processes involved in organizing AVAHAN, including:

- Festival planning and coordination
- College and participant registration
- Event and team management
- Scheduling and venue allocation
- Result and score management
- Points and championship calculations
- Certificate generation and distribution
- Announcements and communication
- Storage and sharing of festival-related data

The research will focus on understanding the current process rather than designing the future software system.

## 4. Research Participants

Where possible, information will be collected from people involved in different parts of AVAHAN, such as:

- Core organizing committee members
- Faculty coordinators
- Student coordinators
- Event coordinators
- Registration team members
- Result and scoring team members
- Participating college representatives
- Participants

Not every stakeholder may require a formal interview. The goal is to gather perspectives from the roles necessary to understand the complete workflow.

## 5. Key Research Questions

The research should answer questions such as:

- What happens from the beginning of festival planning until the festival is completed?
- Who is responsible for each major activity?
- What information is collected at each stage?
- Where is that information stored?
- How is information transferred between people and teams?
- Which tasks are manual, repetitive, or time-consuming?
- Where do errors, delays, confusion, or duplicate work commonly occur?
- Which existing tools and processes work well and should be preserved?
- What information do organizers need during and after the festival?

## 6. Research Method

Information about the current system will be collected through:

- Interviews with AVAHAN organizers
- Discussions with event coordinators
- Observation of existing workflows
- Review of existing forms, spreadsheets, documents, and communication methods

## 7. Important Principle

The current workflow must not be assumed.

For example, the current process might involve a combination of tools and methods such as:

WhatsApp → Google Forms → Excel Sheets → Paper Forms → Phone Calls

However, this is only a hypothesis until it is confirmed through research.

## 8. Expected Research Outputs

The research phase will produce the following documents:

- `interview-guide.md` — Questions used during interviews
- `interview-summary.md` — Findings from interviews
- `current-system.md` — Description of how AVAHAN currently operates
- `current-workflow.md` — Step-by-step current workflow
- `pain-points.md` — Problems and inefficiencies discovered during research
- `stakeholder-map.md` — People and roles involved in organizing AVAHAN
- `tools-and-data.md` — Existing tools, documents, spreadsheets, forms, and data storage methods

## 9. Research Outcome

The final outcome of this research phase is a documented and evidence-based understanding of how AVAHAN currently operates, including its stakeholders, workflows, tools, data, and pain points.

These findings will provide the foundation for defining product requirements, identifying improvement opportunities, and making informed technical decisions.
